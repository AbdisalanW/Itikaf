Privacy Policy

Itikaf exists so you can speak freely about what's on your heart. That only works if you can trust what happens to your words. Here's exactly what we do and don't do.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with data brokers, advertisers, or any third party for marketing purposes — no exceptions.
  • We do not use ad SDKs, analytics SDKs, or location-tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not keep your voice recordings on our servers. Audio is sent for transcription and discarded as soon as the transcript comes back — it is never written to storage. Your own copy of the recording stays only on your phone.

What we do

  • When you record an entry, the audio is sent to OpenAI's Whisper service to be turned into text. The text of your entry (spoken or written) is then sent to Anthropic's Claude to check for signs of crisis, choose a relevant verse, hadith or dua, and write a short reflection. Both providers process it only to return a result to us, under terms that don't allow them to train on it. We don't log your entry's text.
  • Your journal text is then encrypted on your own device before it is stored, using a key that stays on your device. We cannot read your stored journal text.
  • We do store, unencrypted and linked to your account: the short reflection written for you, which verse, hadith or dua was matched, a few theme tags (such as "grief" or "gratitude"), the detected language, and whether the entry was flagged as a possible crisis. This lets you reopen past reflections. It's deleted with your account.
  • If your entry suggests you may be in crisis, we show you crisis resources (such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) instead of a normal reflection. Itikaf is not a substitute for a qualified scholar or mental health professional.

Finding a therapist

  • If you search for therapists, the ZIP code you enter is sent to the U.S. government's public NPI Registry to look up licensed providers near it. We don't save your ZIP code on our servers; the app remembers it on your phone so you don't have to retype it.
  • Links to therapist directories open those websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Subscriptions

  • If you subscribe to Itikaf Premium, your payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your card details.
  • We use RevenueCat to keep track of whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your subscription status and your Itikaf account ID (a random identifier, not your email), but never your journal entries or anything you write.

Your control

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings.
  • Because the encryption key lives only on your device, if you delete the app or switch phones, your past journal text can no longer be decrypted — by you or by us. Your reflections and matched verses remain visible.
