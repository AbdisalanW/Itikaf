Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Open Itikaf, go to Settings, and tap "Delete account." This permanently removes your account and all your reflections.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, and select Itikaf.
  • Is my journal private? Yes — your journal text is encrypted on your own device before it's stored, and your voice recordings are only kept on your phone. Audio is sent securely for transcription and isn't stored on our servers. See our privacy policy for details.
  • Where do the therapist listings come from? From the U.S. government's public NPI Registry of licensed providers. Itikaf doesn't endorse any provider, and the registry doesn't list faith, culture or languages — the Muslim therapist directories linked in the app can help with that.
